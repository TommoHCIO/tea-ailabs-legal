Privacy Policy

Tea AiLabs · Last updated: 26 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tea AiLabs (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with our content channels, websites, and developer applications that connect to third-party platforms (including but not limited to YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram) for publishing and managing our own media.

1. Who we are

Tea AiLabs is a science and space education content brand operating the YouTube channel @tea47-13 and related social accounts. Contact for privacy questions: use the contact options on our public channel or social profiles.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may process:

  • Account connection data — when we (or a tool we authorize) connect to a platform API (e.g. OAuth login), we receive tokens and basic profile identifiers that the platform provides so we can post or manage content on our own accounts.
  • Content metadata — titles, descriptions, schedules, and analytics that platform APIs expose for accounts we control.
  • Public engagement — comments, likes, and other public interactions on our published content, as visible on those platforms.
  • Technical logs — standard server or tool logs (timestamps, error codes) when using developer apps or hosting these legal pages. These pages themselves do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

We do not operate a consumer signup product that sells personal data. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

3. How we use information

  • To publish, schedule, and manage videos and posts on accounts we own.
  • To authenticate developer apps with platform APIs (OAuth).
  • To measure performance of our own content (views, retention, etc.) via platform dashboards and APIs.
  • To respond to messages or inquiries you send us.
  • To maintain security and debug technical issues.

4. Sharing

We may share limited data with:

  • Platform providers (YouTube/Google, TikTok, Meta/Instagram, etc.) under their terms when we use their APIs or post content.
  • Service providers that help us run tools (e.g. cloud hosting, automation platforms) under contractual or standard terms, only as needed to operate our workflow.
  • Legal requirements if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Retention

OAuth tokens and operational logs are kept only as long as needed to operate connected accounts and apps, or as required by the platform. You can disconnect third-party app access in each platform’s security settings at any time.

6. Your choices

  • Revoke app access in TikTok, YouTube/Google, Instagram/Meta, or other platform security settings.
  • Contact us via our public channel if you have a privacy request related to data we control directly.
  • Platform-held data (e.g. your TikTok profile) is governed by that platform’s own privacy policy.

7. Third-party services

Our content appears on third-party platforms. Their privacy policies apply to your use of those services. Examples:

  • Google Privacy Policy (YouTube)
  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Instagram / Meta Privacy Policy

8. International users

We may process information in countries where we or our providers operate. By interacting with our channels or apps, you understand that processing may occur outside your country of residence.

9. Changes

We may update this policy by posting a new version on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
